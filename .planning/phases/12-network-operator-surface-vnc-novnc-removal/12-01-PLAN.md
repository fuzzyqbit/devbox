<objective>
Open the EC2 security group to RDP `:3389` (gated on `var.allowed_web_cidrs`, mirroring the
existing `:8080` code-server ingress), drop the `:6080` noVNC ingress, and scrub every
`:6080`/noVNC reference from the Terraform surface (outputs + variable descriptions). This is
the network-perimeter half of RDP-09 — additive and non-destructive (the SG swap one
allowlist-gated port for another; the SSM port-forward path in plan 12-02 is complementary
and bypasses the SG entirely).

Purpose: An operator whose CIDR is in `allowed_web_cidrs` (default RFC1918 `10.0.0.0/8`)
reaches the new xrdp desktop directly on `:3389`, exactly as they reach code-server on `:8080`.
The SSM-first posture (no public `:22`) and the all-outbound egress are untouched.
Output: edited `terraform/main.tf`, `terraform/variables.tf`, `terraform/outputs.tf`.
</objective>
