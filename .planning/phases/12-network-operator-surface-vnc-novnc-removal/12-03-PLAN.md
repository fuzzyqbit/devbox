<objective>
Surgically excise the VNC/noVNC stack from the Ansible `desktop` role and the `secrets`
bootstrap, now that Phase 11's xrdp uses the xorgxrdp/Xorg backend (proven independent of all
of it — research 7-point safety proof). This is the destructive core of RDP-11. The single
largest correctness risk is removing something a surviving feature needs: `tigervnc-server`
shares a `dnf` list with gnome-shell/gnome-session/fonts/mesa, and `vnc-password` is the RDP
login password (NOT a VNC orphan). Remove ONLY the VNC/noVNC artifacts; KEEP GNOME, media
tooling, code-server, and the entire RDP-password pipeline (relabel its comments only).

Purpose: No dead VNC config in the next-baked AMI — every vncserver/novnc service, the VNC
PAM file, SecurityTypes=Plain config, the noVNC install, and the tigervnc-server package are
gone, while the GNOME-over-RDP desktop, the media tools, and the ec2-user PAM password (which
xrdp authenticates against) all survive. This includes the BAKED secrets-bootstrap systemd
unit: `devbox-secrets-bootstrap.service.j2` is rendered into /etc/systemd/system in the AMI
(install-oneshot.yml:12-13), so its `Before=` ordering and `Description` are themselves part of
the image and MUST drop the deleted vncserver/novnc unit names.

Output: edited desktop role (tasks/defaults/handlers), three deleted desktop templates,
relabelled secrets role (publish/defaults/bootstrap), the functional bootstrap restart-loop
swap to xrdp, AND the baked secrets-bootstrap unit's Before=/Description swap to xrdp.
</objective>
